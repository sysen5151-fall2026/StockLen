How scores are calculated

StockLens uses 21 aligned adjusted closes to calculate momentum over 20 periods and the sample standard deviation of 20 simple returns. Stronger momentum and lower volatility earn more points.

Each factor contributes up to 50 points using average-tie ranks across the fixed ten-stock universe. News provides context for the selected pair. Scoring uses prices only. The local model identifies the leading factor after scoring.

The published snapshot supports one analysis date. The synthetic observation dates are part of the dataset and do not represent an exchange calendar.
