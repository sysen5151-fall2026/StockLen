Business / Mission Analysis

How this comparison works.

StockLens combines a deterministic score with evidence-linked explanation. It does not execute trades, manage portfolios, or claim predictive accuracy.

01

Inputs

Historical prices, volatility estimates, dated news records, and a user-selected comparison universe.

02

Calculation

Momentum and sentiment add to the score. Volatility reduces it. Factors are normalized only against selected stocks.

03

Explanation

A plain-language summary references the completed score and visible evidence; it never changes the numerical order.

04

Validation

Compare task completion, driver comprehension, and evidence-location success against proposed 80% thresholds.

DEMONSTRATION FORMULAScore = 100 × (w₁·M + w₂·N + w₃·V⁻)

M and N are min–max normalized momentum and sentiment. V⁻ is inverse-normalized volatility, so lower volatility receives more points. Weights total one. These migrated numerical values have no per-value provenance or documented observation windows. Treat them as illustrative inputs until verified. Explanations are deterministic templates, not AI-service responses.
