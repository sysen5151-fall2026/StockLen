How scores are calculated

StockLens uses 21 aligned adjusted closes to calculate momentum over 20 periods and the sample standard deviation of 20 simple returns. Stronger momentum and lower volatility earn more points.

Each factor contributes up to 50 points using average-tie ranks across the fixed universe for the current dataset. News provides context for the selected pair. Scoring uses prices only. The local model identifies the leading factor after scoring.

Imported analysis dates come from the supplied market records. A date is available when every ticker has an adjusted close for each of the 21 supplied dates ending on that date. The sample snapshot has one synthetic date.
