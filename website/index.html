Stock universe and ranking method

The fixed list uses 100 companies from the November 20, 2026 OEF holdings snapshot, with one share class per company. Holdings source.

Rank is based on the absolute difference between a stock’s return and SPY’s return over the same window. The window starts at the last SPY trading close strictly before the publication date and ends at the selected close strictly after it. The publication date uses New York time. The signed difference shows whether a stock outperformed or underperformed the market. Other news and market factors can contribute to the observed moves.
