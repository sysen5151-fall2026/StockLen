News and stock moves

Select news to see how each analyzed stock moved around its publication. Observed returns alone cannot establish what caused a price move.

How scores are calculated

StockLens uses 21 aligned adjusted closes to calculate momentum over 20 periods and the sample standard deviation of 20 simple returns. Stronger momentum and lower volatility earn more points.

Each factor contributes up to 50 points using average-tie ranks across the fixed universe for the current dataset. Scoring uses prices only. News analysis reports returns from the last close strictly before publication day to the chosen close strictly after it, within the selected analysis cutoff. The method brackets the date when only the publication day is known.

Imported analysis dates come from the supplied market records. A date is available when every ticker has an adjusted close for each of the 21 supplied dates ending on that date. The sample snapshot has one synthetic date.
